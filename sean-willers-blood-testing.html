Recommended blood tests

Useful starting points for lifestyle and performance goals.

These tests are commonly relevant for people working on weight loss, energy, alcohol reduction, training performance, recovery and general health. Barry and AIO Medicals will confirm which option is appropriate before booking.

Broad overview Premium Complete Blood Test

A wider profile for people who want a fuller baseline across general health markers.

 Energy Tiredness and Fatigue Blood Test

Useful when low energy, sleep disruption or brain fog is part of the picture.

 Alcohol reduction Liver Blood Test

A practical option for people improving drinking habits and overall wellbeing.

 Heart health Cholesterol Blood Test

Supports conversations around cardiovascular risk, nutrition and lifestyle change.

 Metabolic health Diabetes (HbA1C) Blood Test

Checks average blood glucose, often relevant during weight-loss programmes.

 Nutrition Vitamins Blood Test

Helps identify vitamin markers that may affect energy, diet and consistency.

 Hormones Thyroid Function Blood Test

A focused test for people discussing energy, weight change or metabolism.

 Performance Sports and Fitness Blood Test

Designed for active people who want insight into recovery and performance markers.

 Men’s health Male Hormone and Fertility Blood Test

Relevant for men exploring testosterone, energy, libido or fertility markers.
