What this test can help with

A blood profile for common physical contributors to erectile dysfunction, including hormones and metabolic markers.

Blood tests can support health conversations, but results should be interpreted alongside symptoms, medical history and professional clinical advice.

What may be included

  • Testosterone-related markers
  • Diabetes and cholesterol checks
  • Thyroid or general wellbeing markers where appropriate

Who it may suit

  • Erectile dysfunction concerns
  • Low libido or energy
  • Patients preparing for a clinical review

Before you book

  • Bring identification if requested by the clinic.
  • Tell the team about medicines, supplements or recent illness if relevant.
  • Ask whether fasting, cycle timing or gestation requirements apply.
