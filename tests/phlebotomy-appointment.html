What this test can help with

A professional blood draw appointment for patients who already know which sample or kit they need collected.

Blood tests can support health conversations, but results should be interpreted alongside symptoms, medical history and professional clinical advice.

What may be included

  • Identity check and appointment preparation
  • Blood sample collection by trained staff
  • Packaging or handover according to the agreed pathway

Who it may suit

  • External test kit users
  • Patients referred for a blood draw
  • Patients needing a clinic-based blood sample appointment

Before you book

  • Bring identification if requested by the clinic.
  • Tell the team about medicines, supplements or recent illness if relevant.
  • Ask whether fasting, cycle timing or gestation requirements apply.
